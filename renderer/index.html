Who does Agent tasks

Your rules decide, no AI involved. They're checked top to bottom against the task's words, and the first match wins. Separate words with commas. #local or #claude in a title always wins. Each task shows which rule sent it, and you can switch it any time.

Claude's browser

When an Agent task needs a website, Claude uses its own Chrome window — separate from your everyday Chrome. Open it to watch, or to log in to sites you want Claude to use. It fills things in but asks you before anything final (buy, submit, send, book).

Google Calendar

Tasks with a due date sync to Google Calendar — tasks with a time become timed events, dateless-time tasks become all-day events.
You need a free Google Cloud credential (choose Desktop app as the application type) — takes about 5 minutes to set up.
