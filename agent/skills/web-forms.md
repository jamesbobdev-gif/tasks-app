# Working on web pages

- **One step at a time.** Open the page, read it, do one thing, read what changed. Don't plan ten
  clicks ahead: pages change after each one.
- **Popups first.** Cookie banners, newsletter and "download our app" popups block clicks. Close them
  with Reject / Close / No thanks / ✕ (or Escape). Never accept marketing, sign-ups or notifications.
- **Before filling, read the whole form** (scroll or search the page for "Next", "Continue", "Submit"),
  so you know how many pages it has and which fields are required (marked * or "required").
- **Field formats:** dates as the field shows them (MM/DD/YYYY in the US); phone as 10 digits unless
  the page shows a format; states and countries picked from the dropdown, not typed.
- **Fields that suggest as you type** (city, school, company, address): type the start, wait for the
  list, then click the right suggestion. Typed text alone often doesn't count.
- **Error messages** ("invalid", "required", red text next to a field): fix that field and try again.
  The same error twice means stop and say what the page wants.
- **Multi-page forms:** "Next", "Continue" and "Save and continue" are fine. The last button that sends
  it (Submit, Apply, Place order, Pay, Book) is a final action: stop there and ask him.
- **Job portals** (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Handshake, iCIMS): they often ask to make an account or
  upload a resume first. Making an account needs his yes and his password, so stop and ask. Fill the
  questions from his profile only; eligibility, salary and demographic questions get his answer, or
  "Decline to answer" when that choice exists and he hasn't said otherwise.
- **Stop and ask** for: logins and passwords, CAPTCHAs ("I'm not a robot"), two-factor codes, card
  or bank details, file uploads, and anything you'd have to guess.
- **Report what the page shows**, not what you meant to do: list what's filled, what's still empty,
  and the exact button left for him.
